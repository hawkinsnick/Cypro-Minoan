# Rights and Reuse Registry

Rights are tracked independently from scholarly provenance. A source being citable or publicly accessible does not imply that its content may be redistributed.

## Default rule
When rights are unclear, the project stores a citation/link and does **not** redistribute the material.

## Current reusable sources
- Musée du Louvre textual collection entries: Etalab Open Licence, with source/authorship and latest-update acknowledgement required.
- Unicode data files: generally Unicode License v3 (SPDX: Unicode-3.0), subject to the license attached to the specific upstream file.
- Unicode characters/code points may be used without special permission.

## Explicit exclusions
- Louvre photographs are not treated as Etalab textual data.
- Unicode chart glyph artwork is not extracted or redistributed.
- Olivier/Ferrara editorial transcriptions, drawings, photographs, and substantial protected text are not redistributed absent a verified applicable permission/license.
