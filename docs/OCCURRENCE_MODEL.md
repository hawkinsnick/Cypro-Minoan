# Sign occurrence model — v1.2.0

An object may contain multiple marked zones. A zone may be an inscription, potmark, numeral, separator, or uncertain mark. Occurrences belong to zones, not merely objects. TIRY Avas 001 demonstrates why: its two-sign inscription and separate one-sign potmark are explicitly distinguished by the editors. Occurrence data is admitted only with source-level verification; web photographs are not silently transcribed.
