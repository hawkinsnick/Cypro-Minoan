# Architecture 5.0 — evidence graph and question-specific research readiness

5.0 does not declare the corpus globally analysis-ready. It makes readiness a property of a question, dataset snapshot, corpus policy and experiment gate.

## Chain of accountability
claim → experiment/direct structural evidence → readiness profile + gate → dataset snapshot → evidence graph → assertion → source → lineage → rights.

## Evidence graph
The graph is a navigational/audit representation of native evidence. It cannot manufacture assertions, merge script identities, or bypass experiment gates. Native records remain authoritative.

## Snapshot identity
Release-scoped snapshot identifiers are immutable evidence identities. Changed evidence receives a new identifier and may declare `supersedes`; old identifiers are not silently redefined.

## Independence
Source count is not witness count. Unknown lineage is not demonstrably independent. Shared-upstream witnesses collapse for independence counting unless claim-level evidence justifies otherwise.

## Comparative boundary
Graphic, structural, distributional, functional, historical, phonetic, numeric and encoding relationships remain typed. No relation is transitive by default. Graphic/historical relations imply no phonetic value.

## Rights
Rights attach to representations, not merely objects or sources. Public availability is not redistribution permission.

## 5.0 non-goals
No claim of complete corpus coverage, complete transcription/occurrence population, complete palaeography, decipherment, language identification, phonetic proof, or corpus-wide frequency validity. Those remain evidence-dependent.
