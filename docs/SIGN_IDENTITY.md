# Sign Identity Policy — v0.2.0

## Three layers
1. **Graphical occurrence** — a mark on a particular object.
2. **Project sign concept** — a revisable grouping of occurrences (`CMSIGN-...`).
3. **External representation/classification** — Olivier-style CM number, Unicode character, or another scholar's sign-list identifier.

No pair of layers is automatically identical.

## Unicode
The registry `unicode-14plus.json` enumerates the 99 encoded Cypro-Minoan characters U+12F90–U+12FF2. Unicode states that this repertoire covers CM1/CM2/CM3, excludes CM0, and is largely based on Olivier 2007. Its glyphs are normalized. Accordingly the registry is interoperability data, not a palaeographic inventory.

## Variants
Variants must be recorded as sourced assertions. Similarity alone does not justify merging signs. Conversely, a different ductus does not automatically create a new project sign.

## Cross-script comparisons
Linear A, Linear B, and Cypriot correspondences belong in concordances and must state whether the claim is graphical, genealogical/historical, or phonetic. Those relationship types must never be conflated.
